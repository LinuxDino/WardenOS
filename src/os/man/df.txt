NAME
  df - free space on the drives

SYNOPSIS
  df [-h] [-k] [path]

DESCRIPTION
  Shows the size, used and free space of the computer's hard drive, of every floppy disk in a disk drive, and of the read-only ROM, each with a usage bar (green, yellow over 70%, red over 90%). With a path, only the drive that holds it is shown. On narrow screens each drive takes a few lines.

OPTIONS
  -h  human readable sizes (the default)
  -k  sizes in KB
  --help  show the usage

EXAMPLES
  df
  df /disk
