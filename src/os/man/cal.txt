NAME
  cal - show a calendar

SYNOPSIS
  cal [-m] [[month] year]

DESCRIPTION
  Shows a calendar of the current month with today highlighted. Give a month and a year to see another month, or just a year to see January (or this month, for the current year). Weekends are dimmed.

OPTIONS
  -m  weeks start on Monday
  --help  show the usage

EXAMPLES
  cal
  cal 12 2026
  cal -m 2 2024
