NAME
  seq - print a sequence of numbers

SYNOPSIS
  seq [-s sep] [-w] [first [step]] last

DESCRIPTION
  Prints the numbers from first (default 1) to last, counting by step (default 1, may be negative or a decimal).

OPTIONS
  -s sep  put sep between the numbers instead of new lines
  -w      pad with leading zeros to the same width
  --help  show the usage

EXAMPLES
  seq 5
  seq 0 5 100
  seq 10 -1 1
  seq -s , 3
