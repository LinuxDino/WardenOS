NAME
  man - manual pages

SYNOPSIS
  man [-k [word]] <command>

DESCRIPTION
  Shows the manual page of a command. WardenOS pages are plain text files in /os/man/<command>.txt; for CraftOS programs the built-in help topic is shown, and for any other program its --help. Long pages open in a pager: up/down or j/k scroll a line, space and b scroll a page, g and G jump to the start and end, q or Ctrl+T quits.

OPTIONS
  -k word  list the commands whose short description contains word (all of them without a word)
  --help  show the usage

EXAMPLES
  man grep
  man -k
  man -k rednet
  man man
