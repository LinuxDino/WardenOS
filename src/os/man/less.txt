NAME
  less - page through a file

SYNOPSIS
  less [-N] <file>

DESCRIPTION
  Shows a file one screen at a time. Up/down or j/k scroll a line, space or f and b scroll a page, g and G jump to the start and end, the mouse wheel scrolls too. q or Ctrl+T quits. Files that fit on the screen are simply printed. more is the same.

OPTIONS
  -N  number the lines
  --help  show the usage

EXAMPLES
  less /os/kernel.lua
  less -N notes.txt
